President’s Message
A message from Mr. Kiran K. Majmudar, President of BCCI Bharuch.
 
Mr. Kiran K. Majmudar
President, BCCI Bharuch
Empowering Industry, Enriching the Nation
It is a matter of immense pride to stand at the helm of the Bharuch Chamber of Commerce and Industry. As we look across our horizon, we don’t just see factories and chimneys; we see the beating heart of India’s economic engine.
As a lifelong citizen of Bharuch, I have walked alongside our industries for over 35 years. Our core determination at BCCI is to be a unified platform — removing bureaucratic hurdles, adding value to every industry from MSMEs to global conglomerates, and ensuring the “Voice of Bharuch” is heard at the highest levels of policy-making.
Whether you are an established giant or an emerging entrepreneur, we provide the platform where your challenges find solutions and your ambitions find scale.
“When Bharuch grows, India grows.”
Jai Hind!

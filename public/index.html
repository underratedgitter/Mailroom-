          Settings

          Mailroom sends through your own SMTP account, so mail comes from your address and lands in your sent folder.

          Mailbox (SMTP)

          Must match the port. Gmail and most providers: 465 with implicit TLS, or 587 with STARTTLS.

          The account Mailroom signs in as. A relay on this machine needs none.

          Gmail needs an App Password, not your login password.

          What recipients see. Defaults to the mailbox address.

          Where replies go, and where unsubscribe requests are mailed. Defaults to the From address.

          An https:// page you host; {email} becomes the recipient. Without one, the unsubscribe link mails the Reply-To or From address.

          Ceiling across all runs; 0 turns it off. Blank keeps the default of 500.

          Mailroom is only reachable from this computer.
